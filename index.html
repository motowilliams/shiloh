Elite Protocol

Strength & Structural Mass Program

🍽️ Nutrition Strategy

Coach's Rule: Never eat until you are painfully full. Eat smaller, heavily calorie-dense items frequently, and drink your calories to bypass stomach volume. Aim for a ~300-400 calorie daily surplus for 0.25-0.75 lb/week mass gain.
Female Athlete Focus: Ensure adequate Iron (beef, spinach) and Calcium/Vitamin D (milk, yogurt) for bone health. Do not skimp on carbs—they fuel explosive throws.

Breakfast

  • Option 1: 1 large heavy bagel (e.g., Dave's Killer Bread) + thick peanut butter + 1 glass whole milk.
  • Option 2: 2 scrambled eggs (cooked in butter) + avocado + whole milk.

Mid-Morning "Grazing"

  • Handful of dense trail mix (walnuts, almonds, dried cranberries, dark chocolate chips).
  • Keep a bag in your backpack and graze between classes.

Lunch

  • Eat: 1 large PB&J (heavy on the PB) on dense bread + 1 whole chocolate milk.
  • Alternative: 1 cup pasta tossed in olive oil & pesto + dark meat chicken (thighs).

Pre & Post Workout

  • Pre (1 hr prior): 1 handful of dates/dried mango + spoonful of peanut butter.
  • Post (Within 1 hr): 16 oz whole chocolate milk (easy digesting liquid calories).

Dinner & Pre-Bed

  • Dinner: 4-5 oz fatty protein (steak, salmon, thighs) + roasted potatoes tossed in olive oil.
  • Pre-Bed: 1 cup Full-Fat Greek Yogurt + 1 tbsp heavy cream + honey & walnuts. (Or half a smoothie).

🥤 The "Thick Tank" Smoothie

Liquids bypass stretch receptors. Drink this once a day to guarantee your surplus.

  • 12-16 oz Whole Milk (or Oat Milk)
  • 1 large ripe banana
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream or coconut cream (Massive calorie boost)
  • 3 heaping tbsp peanut or almond butter
  • 1 scoop whey protein (chocolate or vanilla)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (You won't taste it)
  • 1 big squeeze of honey

📈 Progression & Cycles

Golden Rule: If technique breaks down, don't increase the weight. Increase loads slightly (+2.5-5lb upper, +5-10lb lower) only when you complete every set with excellent form and ~2 reps left in the tank.

4-Week Training Cycle

  • Week 1: Establish technique. Moderate weights. (RPE 6-7)
  • Week 2: Add a small amount of weight. (RPE 7)
  • Week 3: Small additional increase. (RPE 7-8)
  • Week 4 (Deload): Reduce lifting volume by 30-40%. Keep explosive work, reduce fatigue. Restart cycle.
